How do I share my subscription with family?

 

Add up to four more individual logins at no additional cost. Share your subscription with family and friends. It's your choice.

1

 

Go to your MyAccount page using your favorite browser. Tap your MyAccount web page address below.

 
The Ann Arbor News. myaccount.mlive.com/aa
 
The Bay City Times. myaccount.mlive.com/bc
 
The Flint Journal. myaccount.mlive.com/fj
 
The Grand Rapids Press. myaccount.mlive.com/gr
 
Jackson Citizen Patriot. myaccount.mlive.com/jp
 
Kalamazoo Gazette. myaccount.mlive.com/kg
 
Muskegon Chronicle. myaccount.mlive.com/mc
 
The Saginaw News. myaccount.mlive.com/sn
 
 
 

2. If already signed in, Sign Out before proceeding.

 
 

3. Click the big 'REGISTER' button.

 
 

4. Click the big 'CONTINUE' button.

 
 

5. Enter the email for each account.

 

6. Set up a password for each account then click 'Create Account'.

 
 

7. Select a primary look up option from the pulldown AND enter it below.

 

8. Select a look up option from the pulldown AND enter it below.

 
 

9. Click 'Link Subscription.'

 

10. Don't forget to share this information with that family member.

 
  Send an email.
