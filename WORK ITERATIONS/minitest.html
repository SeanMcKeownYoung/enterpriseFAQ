How do I open the newspaper

 
 

Desktop

 
 

1

 

Open your web browser, such as Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox on your desktop or laptop computer or your mobile device or tablet.

 
 

Desktop

 
 
 

Mobile

 

2

 

Locate the address bar. It’s most often at the top of the web page in the browser.

 
 

Desktop

 
 
 

Mobile

 

3

 

Enter the web address of your online newspaper. Select from this list. You can copy from here and then paste into your web address bar or simply click on the button and you'll be taken there. If you're pasting the web address, be sure to click Return, Enter or the arrow button. This will prompt the browser to go to that address.

Save a bookmark.

 

For faster access next time, we recommend bookmarking the website or saving it to your favorites.

 

The Daily Email.

 

Another easy option is to use the daily email you receive when the latest edition is ready. Just open the email and select the link to go directly to the day’s newspaper. If the email is delayed or you can’t find it, you can always go straight to the website instead.
