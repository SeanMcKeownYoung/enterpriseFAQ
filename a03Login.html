How do I log in?

 

Accessing your subscriber content is quick and easy.   Start by choosing whether you'd like to log in through your favorite browser or through our app.
 

Through your browser.
 
 

1

 

Go to your online newspaper. Find yours from the list below. You can copy and paste from here or simply tap the web address and you'll be taken there.

 
The Ann Arbor News. reader.aa.mlive.com
 
The Bay City Times. reader.bc.mlive.com
 
The Flint Journal. reader.fj.mlive.com
 
The Grand Rapids Press. reader.gr.mlive.com
 
Jackson Citizen Patriot. reader.jp.mlive.com
 
Kalamazoo Gazette. reader.kg.mlive.com
 
Muskegon Chronicle. reader.mc.mlive.com
 
The Saginaw News. reader.sn.mlive.com
 

Desktop

 
 

2

 

Click ‘Sign in’ in the top-right corner of the page
 
 

Mobile

 
 

2

 

Tap ‘Sign in’ at the top-right corner of the screen.

 
 

3

 

The ‘Sign in’ page will open. Enter the email address and password linked to your subscription.

 

4

 

Then tap the box for ‘I’m not a robot.’

 

5

 

Tap ’Sign In.’
