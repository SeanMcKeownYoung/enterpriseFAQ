Print. How to.

The online newspaper has an archive of past editions. To find a story, use the ‘Search’ feature located in the upper right toolbar of the online newspaper.
  • Click Search. Find the 'Search' at the top of the screen. If you're on a mobile device, you'll see three bars in the upper left. Click to open.
  • The Search tool will open a dialog box. Enter your search term. You can choose 'Most Recent' or 'Most Relevant'. Then, select the time period for the search: All time, Past day, Past week, Past month, or Past year.
  • Articles with your search term will show along with their publication date and time. Click on any story to view it.
Video. How to.
Print. How to.

Create a library of your favorites. Click on this button to save a page or article for reference later.
  • Saving Pages. In Page View, click on the 'Save' button to save a page for reference later. The button will be shaded black once the Save is complete. You can access Saved pages or articles in the Saved tab in the upper right toolbar.
  • Saving Stories. With the story open, click on the 'Save' button to save the article for later. The button will be shaded black once the Save is complete. You can access Saved pages or articles in the Saved tab in the upper right toolbar.
  • To remove pages or articles, click on the saved button again to remove it from your Saved Content.
