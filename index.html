How do I
register/sign in?

You may be asked to sign in. Use the same email address and password you created for your subscription. Once signed in, your newspaper will open.
VIDEO. How to.
PRINT. How to.

If you're a Subscriber, follow these simple steps:
  1. On your device, open the internet, (like Safari, Chrome, or Edge)
  2. At the top of the screen, tap the long white bar
  3. Type: myaccount.mlive.com (or your local newspaper myAccount page listed here...)
  4. Tap Go or Enter to open the page.
  5. Click on “REGISTER.” Once registered, your subscription will be activated and you’ll have immediate access to the online newspaper. Remember you can add up to four additional family or friends email addresses!

PRINT. How to.

Through the browser, please ensure you sign in at the top-right corner of the webpage with your subscription email address and password.
Through the App, click on the menu on the top-right corner to sign in under “Account”.

How do I
access the paper?

Follow these simple steps:
1. On your device, open the internet (like Safari, Chrome, or Edge)
2. At the top of the screen, tap the long white bar
3. Type: reader.pub.market.com (or your local version like reader.gr.mlive.com)
4. Tap Go or Enter
That’s it—your newspaper will open.
