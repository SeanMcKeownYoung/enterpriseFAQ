How do I get access to the online newspaper?

 

Setting up and registering your account is quick and easy.   Simply log in from your phone, tablet, or computer.   Follow the steps below to get started.
 
 
 

1

 

Go to your MyAccount page using your favorite browser. Tap your MyAccount web page address below.

 
The Ann Arbor News. myaccount.mlive.com/aa
 
The Bay City Times. myaccount.mlive.com/bc
 
The Flint Journal. myaccount.mlive.com/fj
 
The Grand Rapids Press. myaccount.mlive.com/gr
 
Jackson Citizen Patriot. myaccount.mlive.com/jp
 
Kalamazoo Gazette. myaccount.mlive.com/kg
 
Muskegon Chronicle. myaccount.mlive.com/mc
 
The Saginaw News. myaccount.mlive.com/sn
 
 
 

2

 

Click the ‘REGISTER’ button.
 
  
 
 

3

 

Click the ‘CONTINUE’ button.
 
 
 

4

 

Enter your email address. This will be used as your username.
 

5

 

Create a password that you'll remember.
 

6

 

Click the ‘Create Account’ button.
 
  
 
 

7

 

Select a primary look up option from the pulldown and enter it.
 

8

 

Select a look up option from the pulldown and enter it.
 

9

 

Click the ‘Link Subscription’.
 
 
 

Success!

 

Feel free to explore all that your online newspaper has to offer!
