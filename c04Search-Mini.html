How do I search for Articles?

 

The online newspaper includes an archive of past editions,  making it easy to look for stories that have already been published.

Open today’s edition of your online newspaper. Find your online newspaper from the list below. You can copy and paste from here or simply tap the web address and you'll be taken there. OR Tap the Magnifying Glass/Search icon to the right of the online newspaper to go directly to the Search Window.

Look for the Search button in the upper-right toolbar of the online newspaper home page and select it. You can access the Search button as well while navigating any edition.

 If you’re using the app, tap the three bars ☰ in the upper-right corner to open the ‘Settings’ menu. From the the ‘Settings’ menu select ‘Search’.

 
 

2

 

The Search tool will open a dialog box. Enter the word, name, or topic you want to search for. Then select the Enter key.

Choose whether you want results sorted by Most Recent or Most Relevant. You can also select a time period, such as All time, Past day, Past week, Past month, or Past year.

 

3

 

You’ll see articles that include your search term, along with the publication date and time. Select any story to open and read it.
