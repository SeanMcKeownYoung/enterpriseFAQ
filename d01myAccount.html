How do I get to ‘myAccount.’?

 

From the ‘myAccount’ Subscriber Center you can Sign in, Register to activate your account access, create your account log in or an additional log in to share with family.
 
 

1

 

Open your web browser, such as Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox on your desktop or laptop computer or your mobile device or tablet.

Then, locate the address bar. It’s at the top of the web page in the browser.
 
 
 

2

 

Enter the web address of the Subscriber Center. Find yours from the list below. Then click return OR simply click on the button in this list.

 
The Ann Arbor News. myaccount.mlive.com/aa
 
The Bay City Times. myaccount.mlive.com/bc
 
The Flint Journal. myaccount.mlive.com/fj
 
The Grand Rapids Press. myaccount.mlive.com/gr
 
Jackson Citizen Patriot. myaccount.mlive.com/jp
 
Kalamazoo Gazette. myaccount.mlive.com/kg
 
Muskegon Chronicle. myaccount.mlive.com/mc
 
The Saginaw News. myaccount.mlive.com/sn
 
 

3

 

On the log in screen, enter the email address connected to your account along with your password. Enter ‘Sign in.’
